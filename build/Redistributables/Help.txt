This folder contains tools required for the main program to function.

- yt-dlp is used for fetching video metadata and downloading YouTube content.
- FFMPEG is used for modifying downloaded content and converting files.
- AlbumArtDownloader is used for fetching album covers.

You can download each of these programs from the links inside their folders.
yt-dlp is automatically kept up to date, and you don't have to do anything.
FFMPEG and AlbumArtDownloader are not, but if you're having issues with either you may re-download them.

Get Info.cmd provided by https://github.com/npocmaka/batch.scripts/
